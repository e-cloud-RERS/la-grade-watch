Data: LA County Environmental Health – Restaurant and Market Inspections and Violations. The County publishes these quarterly; this app checks for new data weekly. Pasadena, Long Beach and Vernon run their own health departments and are not included. A “change” means a facility's grade differs from its previous graded inspection.
